# Evolution milestones

A partner’s full chain fits within **72 total awake hours**, including its 30-second hatch, when its level requirements are met. Three-form families have a first milestone at 36 hours and a final milestone at 72; two-form families evolve at 72. Only the selected partner accumulates time. Sleep, pause, background/unfocused windows and closed games do not count. Evolving preserves total time and level.

Ordinary Gen 1 level thresholds come from the [Pokémon Database chart](https://pokemondb.net/evolution#evo-g1). Former stone and trade routes now require **level 36**; those are custom conversions, not chart level requirements. No items, trading or happiness checks are needed. Later generations and regional forms are excluded.

Partners start at level 5; 20 lifetime battle XP adds one level. Battle wins grant 20–50 XP; losses grant 8. If a level is reached early, wait for its awake-time milestone. If time is reached first, keep battling. Press Evolve when both are met. Eevee’s choice is permanent within a save.
